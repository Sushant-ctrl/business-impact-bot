Delivery disruption & recovery

RecoverRoute

When a truck breaks down or a road closes, RecoverRoute shows which kirana stores will run out first, who should get the limited stock, and which other suppliers can deliver in time. Every costly decision stays with a person.

The problem

A late truck means empty shelves and lost sales

Retailers

Find out too late

Kirana stores learn about delays at the last minute and can't tell if they'll run out before the goods arrive.

Distributors

Decide by phone

When stock is short, who gets it is decided over calls and guesswork instead of who needs it most.

Both

Don't know who else can supply

Other suppliers exist, but nobody can quickly compare their stock, price and delivery time.

What it does

Two portals, one live backend

Distributor portal
  • Report a disruption in plain words; AI drafts the reason, new ETA and a message to the retailer
  • Allocate scarce stock to the retailer with the fewest days of stock first
  • Partial deliveries, alternative suppliers, retailer network view
Retailer portal
  • Stockout risk on every open order
  • What should I do?: wait, cancel, or order from a ranked alternative supplier
  • Shelf stock tracking, distributor comparison, UPI QR payments

Both portals also have chat on each order, live notifications, and an AI assistant that answers questions about your own live data.

Inventory & allocation

12 cartons of cola, 3 orders

Orders are sorted by the retailer's days of stock left (shelf stock ÷ daily sales) and filled in that order. The distributor can override.

Assistant

“Brief me: what needs my attention today?”

Answers from that account's own live orders, stock and requests.

Reliable

Works without AI

If the AI is unavailable, drafting and recommendations fall back to built-in rules, and the UI says which source answered.

Pine Labs AgenticOrg

Agents that recommend, people who approve

Delivery Disruption Assistant

Disruption cards, stockout risk, recovery options and retailer messages.

Stock Allocation & Alternate Sourcing Agent

Allocation by days of cover, supplier ranking, partial deliveries and draft purchase orders. Uses the same rules as the app.

Human in the loop

Shadow mode

Both agents need a person's approval when confidence is below 0.9, and the sourcing agent also for amounts over ₹10,000. They never commit stock or spend money on their own.
